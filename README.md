# Basket Optimizer

**Read the case study → [islamoashraf.github.io/Smart-Basket-Optimizer](https://islamoashraf.github.io/Smart-Basket-Optimizer/)**

A self-directed product design concept for discount grocery e-commerce.

Once the total appears, a cart can handle quantity and removal — but choosing a
different product means leaving the cart and coming back to see the new total.
Basket Optimizer brings those alternatives, and their effect on the total, into
the cart itself.

The case study covers the problem framing, the existing patterns it borrows from
and departs from, the direction, the interaction design across five screens, and
how the idea would be validated.
